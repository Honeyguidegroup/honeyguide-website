ABOUT HONEYGUIDE

A platform for value creation.

HoneyGuide Group Limited is a growing Nigerian enterprise being built around agricultural value addition, food processing, product development, branding, distribution and strategic partnerships. The company’s first operating focus is food and agricultural value addition, with room to develop a broader portfolio as capabilities, evidence and markets grow.

OPERATING IDEA

Connect origin to market.

GROWTH PATH

Build locally. Expand responsibly.

WHAT WE DO

Four connected capabilities.

The company’s current direction begins with food processing, while keeping room for a wider portfolio as capabilities, market evidence and partnerships develop.

01

Agro-Processing

Adding value to agricultural resources through processing, quality, packaging and market access. The wider portfolio may include commodities such as sweet potato, ogbono, cassava and other locally sourced agricultural products where a viable value-addition opportunity exists.

02

Food Manufacturing

Developing consumer products with attention to taste, consistency, food safety and scalable operations.

03

Innovation

Exploring new products, technologies and practical business models that can unlock future value.

04

Partnerships

Working toward relationships with farmers, suppliers, distributors, institutions, investors and development partners.

VALUE CHAIN

From farmer to consumer.

Our first venture is designed around a clear chain: sourcing close to production, controlled processing, professional packaging and distribution into identified markets.

01 · SOURCE

Farmers & raw materials

Identify suitable agricultural supply, source responsibly and manage quality from the beginning.

02 · PROCESS

Food transformation

Wash, peel, slice, dewater, fry or bake as appropriate, season, cool and prepare for packaging.

03 · PACKAGE

Make it market-ready

Develop packaging, product presentation and quality systems suitable for a packaged consumer food.

04 · BRAND

Make value visible

Turn processed commodities into clearly positioned, professionally packaged and branded products that customers can recognise and trust.

05 · DISTRIBUTE

Reach the market

Build retailer, distributor, institutional and direct channels, beginning with the Bayelsa market-entry phase and expanding as evidence supports wider distribution.

06 · EXPAND

Domestic growth, future export

As products, systems and market access mature, HoneyGuide intends to extend distribution beyond its initial territories and pursue suitable international opportunities.
