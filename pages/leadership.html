WHY THE FOUNDER

Preparation is not the same as pretending.

The investment case should be based on what can be demonstrated: a founder with a documented record of learning and responsibility, a defined first product, a clear operating model and a willingness to use specialist expertise where needed.

Jerry Whyte brings an unusual combination of communication training and practical industrial exposure. His academic background supports communication, documentation and analysis. His industrial career exposed him to safety-critical procedures, teamwork, operational discipline and risk awareness. His HSE training strengthened that foundation. His enterprise-development experiences added exposure to business development and competitive idea environments.

The HoneyGuide project does not claim that these credentials alone make the founder a food-processing specialist. Instead, they demonstrate a capacity to learn, work within systems, take responsibility, communicate, document, manage risk and build relationships. Food science, food safety, engineering, regulatory compliance, accounting and other specialist functions will be supported by competent professionals as the company develops.

That distinction is important to investors and lenders: the founder is not asking anyone to finance a résumé. The objective is to build an operating business in which credentials, product evidence, supplier documentation, production trials, market validation and financial controls can progressively reinforce one another.
