FIRST PRODUCT · PRE-COMMERCIAL

Macdon’s
Chips.

Packaged sweet-potato chips designed to turn locally sourced agricultural produce into a convenient consumer snack.

The launch flavour direction is Chili & Lemon, followed by Cheese & Onion. Salted/plain may also be trialled during product development.

The package shown is a concept/display visual. Product formulation, final packaging, shelf life, food-safety validation and regulatory requirements remain part of development.
PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Built around the details.

Macdon’s is not presented as already launched. The current stage is pre-commercial product validation and preparation for controlled market entry.

01

Raw material

Sweet potato is the launch raw material. The wider group may explore other crops in future.

02

Taste & texture

Chili & Lemon is the intended launch flavour, with Cheese & Onion next in the product roadmap.

03

Packaging

Professional consumer packaging is part of the product-development objective and must be validated for the final product.

PRODUCTION PATH

Sweet potato to finished pack.

The intended production flow connects sourcing and food processing with packaging and distribution.

ILLUSTRATIVE PROCESS

Receiving → Sorting → Washing → Peeling → Slicing → Dewatering → Frying → De-oiling → Seasoning → Cooling → Packaging

LAUNCH PHASE

Start in Bayelsa.
Build from there.

The initial operating target is deliberately modest: establish product quality, market presence, retailer/distributor relationships and repeat demand before wider expansion.
