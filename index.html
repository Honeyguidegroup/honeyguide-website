ABOUT HONEYGUIDE

A company built for growth, not just a single product.

HoneyGuide Group Limited is an emerging Nigerian enterprise with a long-term vision to build commercially sound businesses that connect local resources with modern processing, strong brands and wider markets.

Our first major operating direction is agro-processing and food manufacturing, while our broader ambition extends to innovation, productive partnerships and scalable ventures.
