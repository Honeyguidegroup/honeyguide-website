A FORMATIVE BEGINNING

Learning business by doing the work.

Jerry grew up helping his grandmother, Madam Grace, a well-known trader and entrepreneur in Kabiama, Sagbama LGA. Her business connected farmers, commodities and markets: sourcing directly from producers, aggregating and storing goods, preserving some commodities through practical traditional methods, and moving products between markets.

When Madam Grace travelled to Aba to restock, Jerry often remained in the store, helping with sales and attending to customers. He also accompanied her on larger-market trips, learning through real transactions — purchasing, pricing, storage, customer demand and distribution.

Those experiences became his first practical exposure to buying and selling. They helped shape a lasting business principle: find value, understand the market, manage resources carefully, serve people well, and build for growth.
